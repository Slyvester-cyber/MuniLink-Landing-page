The problem

01 / 04

The work was done.
The evidence is somewhere else.

Most audit-evidence problems are not about bad decisions. They are about proof that cannot be found, in order, when the auditor asks for it.

01
SCM award files

Files arrive incomplete

A declaration of interest, a committee appointment or a tax-compliance check is missing when the file is requested.

02
Records

Evidence lives everywhere

Minutes in one inbox, signed copies on a shared drive, originals in a cabinet. Retrieval depends on memory.

03
Approvals

Approved, but not provable

The right person decided, but the delegation, signature or date that shows it is not where it should be.

How a readiness check works

02 / 04

Four steps. One workflow.
A clear next action.

Small on purpose, so it fits around audit season and leaves your team with something concrete to act on.

  1. 01
    ☏

    Discovery call

    20 minutes on Teams or by phone. We agree the workflow, your next audit milestone and who owns the evidence.

    20 min
  2. 02
    ◎

    One bounded workflow

    We take a sample, such as a handful of recent award files, and walk the evidence trail with your team.

    Sample
  3. 03
    ◇

    Evidence map & findings

    Every required record, where it is and whether it is complete, with plain findings that point to source documents.

    Evidence

    Next action

    A prioritised fix list with owners, before fieldwork starts. You decide whether to stop there or go further.
