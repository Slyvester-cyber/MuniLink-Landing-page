SCM Award File Audit Checklist

15 items an Auditor-General (AGSA) audit team commonly looks for in a municipal supply chain management award file. Use it to test one file before the auditors do.

Book a 20-minute call →
Guidance, not legal advice. This checklist summarises common requirements of the MFMA Municipal Supply Chain Management Regulations (2005), the PPPFA and the Preferential Procurement Regulations (2022). Your municipality's own SCM policy, delegations and current National Treasury instructions apply, and regulation numbers in your policy may differ. AGSA forms its own audit approach; using this list does not guarantee any audit outcome.

Before advertising

  1. 01

    Need and budget confirmed

    A documented, approved need for the goods or services, with confirmation that budget was available before the process started.

    Evidence to have on file
    Approved requisition or needs memo; budget confirmation.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 10 (demand management)
  2. 02

    Specification approved before the advert

    The specification was drafted by the bid specification committee and approved by the accounting officer (or delegate) before the bid was published.

    Evidence to have on file
    Specification with approval signature and date; committee minutes.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 27
  3. 03

    Committees properly appointed

    Bid specification, evaluation and adjudication committee members were appointed in writing, and committees met their required composition and quorum.

    Evidence to have on file
    Signed appointment letters; attendance registers per sitting.
    Reference
    MSCM Regs 26–29
  4. 04

    Committee members declared their interests

    Each committee member signed a declaration of interest and confidentiality for this bid, and any conflict was recorded and managed.

    Evidence to have on file
    Signed member declarations for each meeting.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 46; SCM code of conduct
  5. 05

    Right procurement method for the value

    The method used (quotations or competitive bidding) matches the value and your SCM policy thresholds, with no splitting of requirements to avoid a threshold.

    Evidence to have on file
    Value estimate; method justification on file.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 12

Advertising and receiving bids

  1. 06

    Advert ran for the minimum period

    The bid was publicly advertised with a closing date at least 30 days after publication (over R10 million incl. VAT, or long-term) or 14 days otherwise. Any shorter period was justified and approved.

    Evidence to have on file
    Advert copies (eTender portal, website, newspaper) with dates; approval of any shortened period.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 22
  2. 07

    Bids opened in public and recorded

    Bids were opened in public as soon as possible after closing, and recorded in a bid-opening register.

    Evidence to have on file
    Bid-opening register with names, prices and signatures; closing-time record.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 23
  3. 08

    Bidder declarations complete

    The recommended bidder submitted the required, signed declarations, including the declaration of interest (MBD 4 or SBD 4, as your policy requires), past SCM practices (MBD 8) and certificate of independent bid determination (MBD 9).

    Evidence to have on file
    Signed declaration forms in the file.
    Reference
    MSCM Regs 38 & 44; bid documents

Evaluation and award

  1. 09

    CSD registration and tax compliance checked

    The recommended bidder was registered on the Central Supplier Database and its tax matters were verified as in order before the award.

    Evidence to have on file
    Dated CSD report and tax-compliance status at the time of award.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 43; National Treasury CSD requirements
  2. 10

    No award to persons in the service of the state

    Directors, members and shareholders were checked against the state-employment and municipal staff information, and any close-family relationships were declared for disclosure.

    Evidence to have on file
    CSD/ID verification results; disclosure records.
    Reference
    MSCM Regs 44 & 45
  3. 11

    Preference points calculated correctly

    Price and specific-goals points used the correct system (80/20 up to R50 million, 90/10 above, all taxes included), the specific goals were stated in the bid invitation, and claims were supported.

    Evidence to have on file
    Scoring sheets; proof of specific goals claimed (for example a B-BBEE certificate or sworn affidavit where that is a goal).
    Reference
    PPPFA; Preferential Procurement Regulations, 2022
  4. 12

    Evaluation followed the published criteria

    The evaluation committee assessed bids only against the criteria and functionality thresholds stated in the bid documents, and recorded reasons for disqualifications.

    Evidence to have on file
    Signed evaluation report and minutes; functionality score sheets.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 28
  5. 13

    Award approved by the right person

    The adjudication committee's recommendation and the final award were approved by the accounting officer or an official acting within a written delegation. If the award differs from the recommendation, the required notifications were made.

    Evidence to have on file
    Adjudication minutes; signed award approval; delegation register in force at the time.
    Reference
    MSCM Regs 5 & 29

Contract and exceptions

  1. 14

    Contract signed before performance

    A written contract or service-level agreement was signed by both parties before work started or goods were delivered, and recorded in the contract register.

    Evidence to have on file
    Signed contract or SLA; contract register entry; award notice where your policy requires publication.
    Reference
    MFMA s116; MSCM Reg 5
  2. 15

    Deviations properly justified and reported

    Any deviation from the normal process was limited to emergencies, sole suppliers or genuinely impractical cases, with reasons recorded, approved by the accounting officer and reported to council.

    Evidence to have on file
    Deviation memo; accounting officer approval; council report; note in the financial statements.
    Reference
    MSCM Reg 36
